## Formula racing circuit centreline data

The geographic centreline coordinates used to shape the in-game Monza, Interlagos, Marina Bay, Silverstone, Spa-Francorchamps, Suzuka and Monaco layouts are derived from the `f1-circuits` GeoJSON dataset by Tomislav Bacinger.

- Source repository: https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits
- Data file: https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits/blob/master/f1-circuits.geojson
- License: MIT License
- Copyright: (c) 2019-2025 Tomislav Bacinger

The coordinates were converted from longitude/latitude into the game's local coordinate system and interpolated to generate the road geometry. The source repository states that its circuit data is unofficial and is not associated with or endorsed by Formula One or the FIA. This game is independent and is not an official Formula 1 product.
